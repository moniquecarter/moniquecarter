## 🧠 What I Care About

**AI System Assurance** — I believe guardrails in AI systems have to be engineered and not prompted. AI has to be given a narrow scope of permissions and its level of autonomy has to be earned progressively. You would not give a new employee full access to the estate. Demonstrate trust and promote accordingly.

**Leadership & Mentorship** — I love to share what I'm learning with others. I have experience teaching middle-school and high-school students a fun robotics class at Virginia Tech. I led Lego Mindstorm activities for students interested in STEM.

**Societal Impact of AI** — AI being used for hiring decisions, auto insurance quotes, and medical diagnosis can potentially have a negative impact on society. I want to help reduce risks and protect human rights and access to opportunities without bias that can creep into data dependent AI models.
